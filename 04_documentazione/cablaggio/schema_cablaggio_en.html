NanoEls H5 — Wiring schematic

Connectors, pinout and signals with the Italian silkscreen names (ELS controller, firmware H5V65)
Italian rear label
Pins 1…4 = DB2EVC-3.5-4P connector
A4 sheet — print at 100%
Pin 1 is +5V, on the side of the 5V marking on the connector. The PCB has two faces, both silkscreened: on the face with 5V ENA DIR STEP · 5V GND B A · 5V GND CLK DATA, +5V is the first and the pins go 1 → 4; on the other face the markings are STEP DIR ENA 5V · A B GND 5V · KEYD KEYC GND 5V and +5V is the last, so the pins go 4 → 1. Follow the label printed on the face you are looking at: nothing has to be inverted by hand.

Toward the driver the signals come out at 5 V (SN74HCT245 converter). Motor power (VMOT) does not pass through here: it goes to the driver terminals.
Order: +5V · ABIL. · SENSO · PASSO = pins 1 → 4 (face with 5V ENA DIR STEP); on the other face PASSO · SENSO · ABIL. · +5V = pins 4 → 1 (face with STEP DIR ENA 5V).

UART: pin 1 = +5V, pin 4 = TX. Separate connector next to the NanoEls H5 marking, on the short side opposite the ESP32-S3 module; not on the rear label (UART0, shared with the USB monitor).
  • Board silkscreen: ZPULSE / XPULSE / YPULSE = IMPULSI Z/X/Y · ZSCALE / XSCALE / YSCALE = SCALA Z/X/Y · JSIGNAL = SEGN. JOY · JPOWER = ALIM. JOY · POWER = ALIM. 5V · MISC = VARIE · KEYBOARD = TASTIERA · STEP / DIR / ENA = PASSO / SENSO / ABIL. · GND = MASSA · DATA = DATI · JBUTTON = JB.
  • All inputs are 5 V tolerant; the axis outputs already come out at 5 V. The Nextion display connects to the 4-pin UART connector.
  • Default values: handwheels 600 pulses/rev, spindle encoder 1200 pulses/rev. They are changed from the web UI.
  • The joystick is off by default: enable it in SETUP if it is used.
  • The linear scales SCALA Z / SCALA X / SCALA Y are wired on the board but firmware H5V65 does not read them.
  • The rear label lists the signals in the order 5V · ABIL. · SENSO · PASSO (that is, from pin 1 to pin 4) and 5V · MASSA · B · A for the inputs.
Before connecting: ELS off, check that pin 1 is +5V (the side of the 5V marking) and that the driver STEP/DIRECTION/ENABLE inputs are optically isolated. Do not bring motor voltage to the board connectors.
